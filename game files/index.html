How to play

Turn the start word into the goal word, one letter per move.
You have 2 minutes per ladder and as many guesses as you can fit in.
A new ladder is dealt every day at your midnight, and each one has a par — the fewest moves it can be done in.

STONE
Deep pink — the letter is in the goal, in that exact spot.
STONE
Signal orange — the letter is in the goal, somewhere else.
STONE
Eggplant violet — that letter is not in the goal word.
  1. Each new word must be a real word and change exactly one letter from your current word.
  2. The 2:00 clock starts on your first letter — the timer sits in the status bar and turns orange in the last 30 seconds.
  3. Guesses are unlimited: detour as much as you like, but the clock does not stop.
  4. Every word you enter is guaranteed to have a route to the goal — words that can't reach it are rejected.
  5. Finish the ladder to keep your daily streak alive. Running out of time resets it.
  6. PAR is the shortest possible number of moves. Beat it for bragging rights.
  7. Stuck? You get 2 hints per ladder — each one tells you which letter to change and to what.
  8. Finish and tap Show the shortest ladder to see how it was meant to be done.

The start and goal words are the same for everyone and reset daily at 00:00 UTC. Your ladder and streak are saved on this device.

Hints cost time

Each hint takes 15 seconds off the clock. You get 2 per ladder.
